# Executive Summary

## Project Overview

This project analyses the New York City Airbnb market to identify pricing patterns, host behaviour, customer engagement, neighbourhood performance, and market opportunities.

The analysis combines Python, SQL, and business intelligence techniques to transform raw Airbnb listing data into actionable insights.

## Dataset

- Listings: 30,555
- Hosts: 16,474
- Neighbourhoods: 223
- Boroughs: 5
- Reviews: 990,170
- Average Listing Price: $278.33

## Key Findings

### 1. Manhattan Dominates the Market

Manhattan contains the largest number of listings and has the highest average listing price among the five boroughs.

Average prices by borough:

| Borough | Listings | Average Price |
|---|---:|---:|
| Manhattan | 9,771 | $379.62 |
| Brooklyn | 7,263 | $213.55 |
| Queens | 3,638 | $175.16 |
| Bronx | 829 | $152.85 |
| Staten Island | 296 | $143.15 |

### 2. Premium Listings Represent the Largest Price Segment

The Premium segment contains the largest number of listings.

| Price Segment | Listings | Average Price |
|---|---:|---:|
| Budget | 5,548 | $67.84 |
| Standard | 6,700 | $146.93 |
| Premium | 7,146 | $305.06 |
| Luxury | 2,403 | $1,051.14 |

### 3. Entire Homes and Apartments Lead Supply

Entire homes/apartments represent the largest room-type segment with 11,537 listings and an average price of approximately $331.

### 4. Customer Engagement

The dataset contains 990,170 reviews, with an average of 32.41 reviews per listing.

Approximately 21,939 listings have at least one review, while 8,616 listings have no recorded reviews.

### 5. Host Concentration

The market includes 16,474 unique hosts. A relatively small number of hosts manage very large listing portfolios, indicating the presence of professional property managers and multi-property operators.

## Business Implications

The findings suggest that Airbnb's NYC market is highly concentrated around Manhattan and Brooklyn, while premium pricing represents a significant part of the market.

Hosts and Airbnb market teams can use these findings to:

- Evaluate neighbourhood-level pricing strategies.
- Identify high-demand areas.
- Benchmark properties against comparable listings.
- Identify opportunities for professional host support.
- Understand customer engagement across price segments.

## Important Data Limitation

Revenue is not directly available in the public dataset. Therefore, the analysis does not claim actual Airbnb revenue.

Revenue-related analysis is treated as pricing or estimated opportunity analysis rather than reported company revenue.
