# Executive Summary

This project analyzes Amazon product listing data to understand product pricing, discount strategies, customer ratings, and category performance. The objective is to identify actionable business insights that can support pricing decisions, product portfolio optimization, and customer satisfaction.

The analysis includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, feature engineering, business insight generation, and professional visualizations. A total of twelve analytical visualizations were developed along with summary statistics and category-level analysis.

---

# Business Problem

Amazon manages thousands of products across multiple categories. Business teams need to understand:

- Which categories perform best?
- Are discounts influencing ratings?
- Which products receive the highest customer ratings?
- What pricing strategies appear most effective?
- Which categories should receive more attention?

The goal is to transform raw product data into actionable business recommendations.

---

# Project Objectives

- Clean and prepare raw Amazon product data.

# Key Business Insights

### Pricing

Most products are concentrated within a limited pricing range, while premium products form a smaller segment.

### Discounts

Many products receive significant discounts, indicating an aggressive pricing strategy.

### Customer Ratings

Most products maintain ratings above 4.0, suggesting generally positive customer satisfaction.

### Product Categories

Certain categories dominate the marketplace by product count, indicating higher market activity.

### Customer Engagement

Products with a large number of ratings demonstrate stronger customer engagement and visibility.
