# Project Summary

# Amazon Product Analytics

## Project Overview

Amazon Product Analytics is an end-to-end Business Analytics project developed to analyse Amazon product data and transform raw information into meaningful business insights.

The project demonstrates the complete analytics lifecycle, including data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), feature engineering, SQL analysis, business intelligence, and data visualization.

The primary objective is to understand customer ratings, pricing behaviour, product discounts, and category performance to support data-driven business decisions.

---

# Business Problem

Large e-commerce platforms manage thousands of products across multiple categories. Business stakeholders require analytical insights to answer questions such as:

- Which product categories perform the best?
- Which products receive the highest customer ratings?
- How are discounts distributed across products?
- Does pricing influence customer ratings?
- Which products generate the highest customer engagement?
- Which categories should receive greater business focus?

---

# Project Objectives

- Clean and prepare raw Amazon product data.
- Perform exploratory data analysis.
- Identify pricing and discount trends.
- Evaluate customer rating patterns.
- Analyse category-level performance.
- Generate business insights.
- Develop professional visualizations.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Support business decision-making through data.

# Key Business Insights

- Most products have customer ratings above 4.0.
- Product pricing varies significantly across categories.
- Discounts are widely used as a pricing strategy.
- Customer engagement differs considerably between products.
- Some categories consistently outperform others in ratings.

# Business Recommendations

- Promote high-rated products through featured campaigns.
- Review lower-rated products using customer feedback.
- Optimise discount strategies to maintain profitability.
- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing categories.
- Track premium products separately for pricing optimisation.
