# Project Overview

This project analyses Amazon product data to understand pricing strategies, discount behaviour, customer ratings, and category performance. The objective is to transform raw product information into actionable business insights through data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, SQL analysis, visualization, and dashboard reporting.

---

# Business Problem

Amazon offers thousands of products across multiple categories. Business teams need to answer questions such as:

- Which categories perform best?
- Which products receive the highest ratings?
- Do discounts influence customer ratings?
- Which products generate the highest customer engagement?
- How can pricing strategies be improved?

---

# Project Objectives

- Clean raw data.
- Perform exploratory data analysis.
- Generate business insights.
- Analyse customer ratings.

# Business Insights

- Most products maintain ratings above 4.0.
- High discounts are common across multiple categories.
- Premium-priced products represent a smaller market segment.
- Customer engagement varies significantly between products.
- Category performance differs in both pricing and ratings.

---

# Business Recommendations

- Promote highly rated products.
- Improve lower-rated products using customer feedback.
- Optimise discount strategies.
- Focus on high-performing categories.
- Monitor premium products separately.
